# SentinelOne

SentinelOne is the endpoint detection and response platform in the defender storyline. Its agent is present on the Windows workstations and domain controllers. The console helps defenders investigate a beacon, follow related process activity, and act on a ransomware event.

### Singularity endpoint protection

![SentinelOne product mark](/logos/sentinelone.png)

Defenders inspect alerts on affected endpoints and follow process and file details to understand what ran and where it moved. The console also receives selected Nozomi Vantage information so OT events can be compared with Windows endpoint activity. Later in the story, SentinelOne protects and rolls back Windows devices after the simulated ransomware exercise. The topology turns recovered Windows devices blue; the PLC remains highlighted until its own state is addressed.
