# Nozomi Networks

Nozomi Networks provides the defender's OT asset and activity view. The lab combines network observation, endpoint context, active discovery, and a cloud console. Later, Vantage sends selected security information to the SentinelOne console.

### Guardian

![Official Nozomi Guardian sensor mark](/logos/guardian-product.svg)

Guardian observes OT network traffic to build an asset inventory and flag unusual behavior. Students use it to investigate the Siemens PLC after the address-changing exercise. Its view of the device is especially useful when an endpoint log alone does not explain an industrial network change.

[Explore Guardian on Nozomi Networks](https://www.nozominetworks.com/platform/guardian)

### Arc endpoint sensor

![Official Nozomi Arc sensor mark](/logos/arc-product.svg)

Arc runs on the Windows workstations and domain controllers in this lab. It adds host context to the Nozomi platform and can help reveal nearby devices. On the topology, its small blue badge marks those protected Windows systems.

[Explore Arc on Nozomi Networks](https://www.nozominetworks.com/platform/arc)

### Smart Polling

![Nozomi Guardian mark for its Smart Polling capability](/logos/guardian-product.svg)

Passive traffic cannot always reveal every device detail. Smart Polling sends targeted, protocol-aware queries to enrich the asset record. The workshop uses an assigned lab PLC to show the extra information obtained through an active plan.

[Explore Smart Polling on Nozomi Networks](https://www.nozominetworks.com/platform/smart-polling)

### Vantage

![Official Nozomi Vantage product mark](/logos/vantage-product.svg)

Vantage brings visibility from Nozomi sensors into a central cloud console. In the final defender activity, students review site-wide information there and follow the configured flow from Vantage into SentinelOne.

[Explore Vantage on Nozomi Networks](https://www.nozominetworks.com/platform/vantage)
