## Welcome to Hack the Brewery

A brewery is under attack. In this workshop, you will follow an intrusion from the Windows 7 engineering station through the OT environment and into IT. You will also investigate the activity and use the workshop's defense tools to detect, contain, and recover from it.

### Rules of Engagement

- Work only in your assigned CloudShare environment and on the systems identified in each challenge.
- Do not access or interfere with another participant's machines, accounts, or files.
- The SentinelOne console is shared. Use your assigned lab group and act only on your own endpoints.
- Follow the challenge sequence. Some defensive actions can stop later exercises; apply them only when the activity asks you to.
- If a machine, account, or service does not behave as described, contact an instructor before changing the lab setup.

### Training environment

This workshop is a controlled cybersecurity exercise. The supplied tools and samples are for the assigned lab. Do not apply these techniques to systems outside the workshop without authorization.

### Flags, points, and hints

Complete each activity by answering its flag question. The question appears directly above the answer field. Your score is shown on the Scoreboard.

For the scored attack and defense challenges, you start with 10 available points. Hint 1 gives more direction and costs 3 points. The full lab guide includes the detailed steps and screenshots and costs another 3 points. Hint costs are deducted when you solve the challenge.

Read the rules, then acknowledge them using the flag field below.
