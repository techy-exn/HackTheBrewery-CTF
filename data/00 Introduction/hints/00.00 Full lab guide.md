> **Welcome**, our environment is under attack, and we **need your help in defending it**. 

<br>

**Let’s set the scene:**

A production brewery is under attack. The first foothold is the Windows 7 engineering station in OT. From there, the attacker reaches domain controllers, tampers with the virtual Siemens PLC, crosses into IT, steals a PLC backup, and spreads ransomware. If the production systems cannot be trusted or restored, the brewery stops.<br>
You will follow the intrusion and then use the workshop defenses to detect it, contain it, restore Windows systems, and secure access to OT. Every machine and action belongs to your assigned CloudShare lab.

<br>

> Your mission: **trace the attack, protect the brewery, and prove each response with evidence from your lab.**
