---
title: "Hint 1 — More direction"
---

- In FortiPAM, open <code>Log & Report</code> and locate the secret-session video records.
- Find the event marked <code>Video upload finished</code> and open its recording.
- Read the <code>Destination IP</code> in the playback header. The destination also appears as the secret address in the event details.
- Submit only that IPv4 address, without a port, URL, or file name.
