<div class="info">
In the previous activity you used the secret to gain remote access to the engineering station. However, the admin still doesn’t have any visibility about what the user is doing on the target machine. To solve this, you can enable session recording.
</div>
<br>

<div class="defender-container">
    <div class="defender-label">Enable Session Recording</div>
	
**Edit** the current secret and enable session recording.

1. Navigate to the Secret settings
2. Enable Session recording. This will provide the admin with a video recording of the activities performed by the user.
	
![FortiPAM Session Recording Configuration](/images/fortipam_editsecret_recording.jpg)

3. Test the Secret with Session Recording enabled. Use the launcher to access the jumpstation?

    *Note: You should now observer a different behavior when launching starting the remote connection (Secret).*

    When launching you will see a pop-up asking if the PAM is allowed to see this tab. **If you cancel, the RDP will not launch.**
    ![FortiPAM Allow Recording](/images/Fortipam_Screen_Before_Recording.jpg)

</div>
<br>

<div class="info">

Once you’ve entered the RDP-session through FortiPAM you can click **stop sharing** at the top of the browser to end the recording, and in extension also the session. You will also see a timer how long you've been in this session.
	
![FortiSRA Inside RDP](/images/FortiPAM_recording_Video_inside_RDP.jpg)
</div>
