<div class="info">

The **Guardian** of **Nozomi Networks** detects everything **passively**. But sometimes not all the necessary data is transmitted over the network. For that we can use **smart polling**. These are **lightweight active queries** to devices, using the **language** of that specific **device**. Examples are: Siemens Step7, SNMP, Modbus, EtherNet/IP and many more.

Important is that the device **needs to be known** by the Guardian. If it's not known you can wait to have it be **discovered** or **manually** upload it into the Guardian.
	
</div>

<div class="defender-container">
    <div class="defender-label">Smart Poll PLC</div>

We will first need to bring up the PLC before we can get information about it. For this we need to access `Siemens PLC` and `Engineering Station 2`. Both these devices can be accessed with the admin credentials
	
On the Siemens PLC, open the S7-PLCSIM Advanced and fill in the information like seen in the picture. The IP address needs to be exactly the same since that IP is referenced in the PLC configuration file on the TIA Portal.

![Start PLC](/images/StartingPLC.jpg)
	
Time to load in configuration on the PLC. Head over to `engineering station 2`. This will be a Windows 10 device. In the middle of the desktop will find `TIA Portal V20`. Open this application 
	
![Start TIA portal](/images/Click_TIA_Portal.jpg)
	
Next select the **project fromblackouttolockout** and click **open**.

![Open project](/images/TIAPortal_OpenProject.jpg)

It can take a while to open this project. After it has been opened you can **click** the **PLC**.
	
![Open project](/images/TIAPortal_SelectPLC.jpg)

Now right click on the PLC and choose `compile - Hardware and Software`
	
![Compile](/images/TIAportal_CompileToDevice.jpg)
	
After **compiling** right click again and choose `go online`. First you will see that it's loading and after a few seconds it will all become **green** and the **PLC** will be **online**.
	
![Going online - orange](/images/TIAPortal_Online_Loading.jpg)
	
![Going online - green](/images/TIA_Portal_Online_Completed.jpg)

![Device Configuration](/images/Download_To_Device.jpg)

![Device Configuration](/images/TIA_Portal_DoubleClick_DeviceConfiguration.jpg)

<div class="defender-container">
    <div class="defender-label">Smart Poll PLC</div>
	
Every device that has been discovered by the Guardian will be under assets.
	
<div class="info">

Nozomi Networks Guardian collects information by **passively** monitoring network traffic via **SPAN**, **RSPAN**, **ERSPAN**, or by uploading a **trace file** (PCAP). So it can **analyze communications without disrupting operations**.
</div>

Let's create a **smart polling strategy** and execute it on our PLC to see what information we can gather. Afterwards we will see what has been gathered passively and what we got extra with smart polling.

Go inside the Guardian to the `Smart Polling` menu.
![Smart Polling Menu](/images/NN_Guardian_SmartPollingMenu.jpg)
	
Here you will see that so far no plan has been created. Let's create our first Smart polling Plan.
![Smart Polling Menu](/images/NN_Guardian_SmartPolling_NewPlanButton.jpg)
	
When creating the plan, you can see that there are multiple **strategies** you can select (Siemens Step7, modbus, WinRM and many more). You also see that there is an **interval** in how often the plan is exectued. Dependend on strategy you can either use a **query** to get information, or just select the information you want to see. If you leave the query empty he will just try to collect as much as possible.
	
![Smart Polling Empty](/images/NN_Guardian_SmartPolling_NewPlan_Empty.jpg)
	
In our example we are querying a Siemens PLC so we will be using Siemens Step 7 as the strategy. If you want to try others out, you can create more plans. 

![Smart Polling Filled in](/images/NN_Guardian_SmartPolling_NewPlan_FilledIn.jpg)

After having created the plan, we just need to enter the IP address of the device we want to query.

![Adding device to Smart Polling plan](/images/NN_Guardian_SP_Add_nodes.jpg)
	
Next up you can execute the plan.
	
![Execute Plan](/images/NN_Guardian_ExecutePlan.jpg)
	
After a few seconds we can go back to the asset list and see how the data is now enriched.
	
![Enriched Asset](/images/Guardian_Enriched_After_Smart_Polling.jpg)
	
If you hover over the small **i**, you will see how he determined this information. You will see that a lot was already discoverd by the **passive detection engine**.
	
![Hover over i icon](/images/NN_Guardian_little_i_icon.jpg)
	
![Detected Passively](/images/NN_Guardian_Detected_Passively.jpg)

![Detected Actively](/images/NN_Guardian_Detected_Active.jpg)

You will see that **1 asset** also consists out of **1 of more nodes**. If you would focus on a different node of that asset, you will for example see a change in protocos (layer 2 uses different protocols compared to layer 3).
After focusing on IP, it's also interesting to see the hardware components of the device. Here we will be able to identify what slots have been added to the PLC.
	
![Focus on menu](/images/Asset_Focus_On.jpg)

![Focus on IP](/images/Focus_On_IP.jpg)
	
Another intresting part is the `network graph` in the Guardian. The network graph shows **how devices (nodes) communicate with each other**. It lets you see which other nodes a specific device is connected to and exchanging data with.
	
![Network Graph](/images/Asset_Network_Graph.jpg)
	
There is a lot more to discover in the Guardian. Please take a moment to have a look around and ask an instructor if you want to know something more!
	
</div>

Nozomi Networks also has their SaaS console, which is called **Vantage**. With this you can have an **unlimited number of virtual guardians** (sensors) onsite. All these guardians then forwards **metadata, alerts, and analysis results** (not raw traffic by default) to Vantage for centralized **visibility** and **management**.
	
In Vantage you can also make smart polling strategies and it will look very similar compared to the Guardian.
	
What you will notice is that the menu looks very similar. This is because Nozomi has choosen to unify their dashboards on all their products. So no matter which product you buy, the dashboards will look very similar (Vantage will just have a bit more options like their AI assistant).
