# Siemens

The brewery process is represented by a virtual Siemens PLC. The Engineering Station runs the Siemens software used to open the provided project, configure the controller, and observe what happens when its network settings change.

### SIMATIC PLC and simulation

![Siemens S7-PLCSIM Advanced product illustration](/logos/plcsim-product.jpg)

The SIMATIC controller is the OT asset whose state matters to the brewery scenario. Students bring the virtual instance online before the attack exercise, then compare its configured address with the address observed afterward. It is a simulated controller in each assigned CloudShare environment.

[Explore SIMATIC automation on Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/simatic/)

### TIA Portal

![Siemens TIA Portal product illustration](/logos/tia-portal-product.jpg)

TIA Portal is the engineering environment used for the controller project. In the introductory challenge, students open the project, select the PLC, compile or load the configuration, and verify the controller is online before moving to the attack and defender phases.

[Explore TIA Portal on Siemens](https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/tia-portal/)
