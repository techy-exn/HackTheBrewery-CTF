---
title: "Hint 1 — More direction"
---

- Open Nozomi Guardian from the Chrome bookmark and sign in with the workshop SSO credentials.
- In the event list, turn off <code>Group by incident</code> so each PLC change is shown as its own event.
- Open the event whose description reports the PLC IP-address change; do not open the separate name-change or function-code event.
- In the Guardian alert list, read the <code>Type ID</code> for that IP-change row. It is the structured label with a prefix and a colon, not the linked alert UUID, event title, or PLC IP address. The full guide's alert-overview screenshot shows the same column.
