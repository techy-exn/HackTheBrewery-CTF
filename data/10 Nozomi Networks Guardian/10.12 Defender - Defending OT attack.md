<div class="info">

**Nozomi Guardian** is a lightweight, passive sensor that monitors OT network traffic without impacting operations. It learns normal behavior over time to create a baseline, and then detects anomalies and threats by identifying deviations from that baseline.
After the Guardian has detected the changes, he forwards the events (as metadata) towards Vantage. Vantage will then use a webhook to send all the data towards SentinelOne.

</div>

<div class="defender-container">
    <div class="defender-label">Accessing Nozomi Networks Guardian</div>

Head over to Google Chrome and click on the **Nozomi Networks Guardian**

There you will login with your SSO **User credentials** (see <a href="/credentials" target="_blank">Credentials Page</a>)
![NN Guardian Login](/images/Guardian_Login.jpg)
	
The Guardian is configured as a SAML Service Provider (SP), with FortiAuthenticator acting as the SAML Identity Provider (IdP). Authentication is therefore handled by FortiAuthenticator, which sends the SAML assertion back to The Guardian.
	
</div>

<div class="defender-container">
<div class="defender-label">SentinelOne</div>

We have also integrated Vantage with SentinelOne using webhooks. If you want to see how we integrated this, have a look at this <a href="https://wlnt.io/s/4vAWrx" target="_blank">**interactive demo**</a>.

Inside SentinelOne under `Triage > Alerts` you will see all the alerts coming in from Vantage (and other sources) 
![S1 Alerts](/images/Alert_Overview-2.jpg)

The first alert is the device state change.
![S1 Alerts](/images/S1_Device_State_Change.jpg)
	
Another alert is baseline deviation. Inside the alert you also see that there was a new global function code.
![S1 Alerts](/images/S1_Baseline_Deviation.jpg)
	
You will see that both alerts are a bit different. We have created 3 alert rules.
1. VI: which triggers on baseline deviation seen by the Guardian and sent by Vantage
2. SIGN: which triggers on a signature matched at Guardian level and sent by Vantage
3. INCIDENT: which is triggered if Vantage sends an incident towards S1
	
Feel free to take a look inside the SentinelOne console!
